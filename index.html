Our mission
"To make the world's finest research visible — across disciplines, across borders, across every institution."

Volta Academia is an international organization and annual fair with a single mandate: to identify and celebrate exemplary research talent wherever it exists in the world.

Since 2016, we have brought together postgraduate scholars from more than 60 countries for four days of rigorous discussion, cross-disciplinary exchange, and public presentation of research.

About Volta Academia

An international
organization built
on one standard.

Volta Academia is a global organization dedicated to recognizing and elevating exemplary research talent — across every discipline, every continent, and every institution. We exist to make brilliant work visible to the world it deserves to reach.

We believe research is the most important work in the world. Our fair exists to honour it.

Volta Academia was founded in Edinburgh in 2016 by a group of researchers who believed exceptional scholarly work was too often invisible beyond the narrow communities that produced it. We created an international platform where the finest postgraduate minds could present their research to a global audience.

Who we are

Volta Academia is headquartered in Edinburgh and operates as an international non-profit organization, governed by an academic board drawn from partner universities across Europe, North America, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.

Membership is open exclusively to holders of Master's degrees and doctoral qualifications who have published peer-reviewed or formally recognized research.

Our international reach

Volta has welcomed scholars from Oxford, MIT, the Sorbonne, University of Tokyo, University of Cape Town, King Abdulaziz University, the Indian Institutes of Technology, and hundreds more institutions worldwide.

Our principles

What Volta stands for.

01
Scholarly rigour

Every Volta member has demonstrated academic achievement through a postgraduate qualification and published contribution. We hold the bar high because the work demands it.

02
International equity

Brilliant research exists everywhere. Volta actively recruits scholars from underrepresented regions and ensures our international programme reflects the global distribution of excellent work.

03
Cross-disciplinary exchange

The hardest problems do not respect departmental boundaries. We deliberately build a programme that puts AI researchers in conversation with legal scholars, economists with public health scientists.

Our history

Nine years of international scholarship.

2016 — Edition I
Volta is founded in Edinburgh

A one-day symposium gathers 34 postgraduate scholars from 8 countries. The founding principle: only published researchers, only rigorous work.

2018 — Edition III
International expansion begins

Volta moves to the Grand Assembly Hall. Scholars from 22 nations attend. Research fields expand to include computer science and law.

2022 — Edition VII
AI & technology programme launched

A dedicated strand for artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer science research is introduced.

2025 — Edition IX
60+ nations, 120+ scholars

Volta Academia's largest edition. Eight disciplines, four days, one international standard of excellence.

Research disciplines

Eight fields.
One standard
of excellence.

Volta Academia is intentionally cross-disciplinary. We believe the most transformative research happens at the edges of established fields — and our international fair creates the conditions for those collisions to occur.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

AI is the defining research challenge of our era. Volta Academia hosts one of the few international forums where AI researchers engage directly with legal scholars, ethicists, economists, and public health scientists. We welcome work from machine learning theory to applied AI systems to AI governance.

Machine learningLarge language modelsComputer visionRoboticsAI ethicsGenerative AIAI governance
💻 Computer Science

From the foundations of computation to global-scale distributed systems, computer science underpins every domain at Volta. We welcome theoretical and applied work with particular interest in research engaging with social and institutional implications.

AlgorithmsCybersecurityDistributed systemsHCIQuantum computingSoftware engineering
🧬 Medicine & Life Sciences

Clinical and laboratory research sits at Volta alongside social science, law, and computer science. We particularly welcome work bridging computational approaches with biomedical questions and research addressing healthcare equity at a global scale.

Clinical researchGenomicsNeurosciencePublic healthBioethicsDigital health
⚖️ Law & Policy

As technology reshapes the conditions of human life, legal scholarship has never been more consequential. Volta brings international lawyers into conversation with technologists, economists, and social scientists. We welcome comparative, international, and theoretical legal scholarship.

International lawData governanceIP & copyrightHuman rightsAI regulation
📊 Economics & Finance

Volta's economics strand spans macroeconomic theory, development economics, and the rapidly evolving intersection of finance and technology. We are especially interested in research addressing global inequality, sustainable economics, and governance of financial systems in emerging markets.

MacroeconomicsDevelopment economicsFintechIslamic financeSustainability
🌍 Social Sciences

Social sciences provide the human context without which no technical research is complete. Volta's international cohort spans political science, sociology, anthropology, and migration studies — contributing perspectives from every region of the world.

Political scienceSociologyAnthropologyInternational relationsMigration
📖 Literature & Humanities

The humanities are not separate from the sciences at Volta — they are essential to them. Literary scholarship, philosophy, and cultural studies provide the interpretive frameworks that make sense of a world shaped by computation, migration, and political upheaval.

Comparative literaturePhilosophyLinguisticsCultural studiesDigital humanities
🏛️ History & Archaeology

Historical scholarship at Volta is global in scope. We are particularly interested in research that challenges Eurocentric narratives, employs digital methods to illuminate the past, and engages with the ethical responsibilities of the historian.

Global historyArchival studiesHeritageMaterial cultureMemory studies
Your field not listed?

Volta Academia continuously expands its disciplinary scope. Inter-disciplinary applications are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
